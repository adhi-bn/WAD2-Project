# Lechaton Typers

Custom mechanical-keyboard builder: users assemble a keyboard from compatible parts against a target budget.

## Language

**Build**:
A user-assembled keyboard made by choosing one Part in each slot.
_Avoid_: Configuration, custom keyboard

**Part**:
One selectable component for a Build, in exactly one of three slots: barebones, switches, keycaps.
_Avoid_: Component, item

**Barebones kit**:
The base unit of a Build, with its case and fixed internals bundled and priced as one Part. Switches and keycaps attach to it.
_Avoid_: Base, kit

**Compatibility**:
Whether the chosen switches, keycaps, and barebones for a Build fit together. PCB and foam are not separately customisable.
_Avoid_: Validity, match

**Budget**:
The user's target spend for a Build, compared against the total price of its selected Parts.
_Avoid_: Price, cost

**Saved build**:
A named Build retained in its owner's account for later editing, including unfinished drafts.
_Avoid_: Preset

**Lucky draw**:
A one-time per-account prize draw that may award a Voucher.

**Voucher**:
A time-limited percentage or fixed-amount discount awarded by the Lucky draw.
_Avoid_: Coupon

**Exploded anatomy**:
The fixed, scroll-driven visual breakdown on the home surface showing how Keycaps, Switches, and the Barebones kit's bundled internals (plate, PCB, foam, case) stack together in a guided, card-by-card sequence with synchronized 3D layer highlighting, camera focus, and beginner-friendly layer facts.
_Avoid_: Live preview, customiser preview

**Build preview**:
The interactive 3D assembly in the builder where the user's chosen Barebones kit, Switches, and Keycaps fit together part by part.
_Avoid_: Static schematic, 2D diagram
